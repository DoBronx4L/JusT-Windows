Our Services

At Just Windows, we provide expert window cleaning services for homes and businesses. Whether you need a routine cleaning or a one-time refresh, our team ensures streak-free, spotless results every time!


Professional Window Cleaning

From cozy homes to thriving storefronts, we make every window shine. Clean windows, brighten interiors, and leave a lasting impression. Whether it's a residential space or a commercial property, our team delivers a flawless finish with attention to detail.


Interior & Exterior Window Cleaning

We clean both sides of your windows for maximum clarity, removing dirt, dust, and water stains to let in more natural light.


Screen & Track Cleaning

Dirty window screens and tracks collect dust and debris over time. We make sure they're as clean as your glass for a fully refreshed look.


Pricing

At Just Windows, we provide professional window cleaning for homes and businesses, ensuring spotless, streak-free results every time. Our pricing is based on the number of window panes, typically ranging between $8-$10 per pane, depending on size and accessibility.


Booking & Cancellation Policy

Appointments must be scheduled in advance. To reschedule or cancel, we require at least 24 hours notice, or a $50 cancellation fee will apply.
